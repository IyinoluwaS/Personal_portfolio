Solo project · Applied AI · 2026

Ledger Agent

Answers plain-English questions about a finance ledger by writing SQL, checking it with a deterministic guard, running it read-only and repairing its own failed queries. It refuses rather than guess.

My role
Designed and built all of it, including the evaluation.
Outcome
On a 16-question test set, the repair loop lifted accuracy from 62% to 94% under a minimal prompt.
